# F8 · The demo run

2026-10-19. One written sequence for the panel, and a script that runs the program through it, watches what it
says and sends, and keeps an evidence folder per run. **The script is built and tested; the rehearsal on the
device has not been done yet** (see the end).

## Before the demo

1. The device is free: `sudo systemctl stop second-vision` (the boot service holds the camera and the boards, and
   runs the *base* checkout, which does not have the demo track's work). No other copy of the program.
2. Connected: the camera, the glasses board, the control panel, the earphones. The TV on the Pi's HDMI.
3. On the panel: DETECT and DEPTH rockers on, speech on, volume up, strength up.
4. In a terminal on the Pi (the desktop's, or SSH; over SSH the preview window still goes to the Pi's screen):

   ```bash
   cd ~/Projects/Second-Vision/worktrees/kenth
   my_hailo_env/bin/python docs/testing/tools/demo_run.py
   ```

   Only some steps, in your order: `--steps camera,lens`. The table below: `--list`.

## How it runs

- It checks the device is free and which parts are there, starts the program the way the boot service does, and
  waits until the camera picture has been checked once (`Camera view: clear`).
- For each step it shows **what to tell the panel** and **what to do**. Press **Enter as you do it**, before or
  after: the program's reaction counts either way. **s** skips the step, **q** finishes the demo.
- While you talk, the important lines scroll by with their time (`[CHECK]`, `[TTS]`, `[PATTERN]`, `[ALERT]`, ...).
  After each action it ticks what it saw (`OK  "Critical Error: Camera cannot be reached": 0.4 s`) and moves
  on as soon as everything expected has been seen, or says `MISS` when its time is up.
- After each step it asks what you saw, heard and felt; type a few words, or Enter to skip.
- At the end (or on **q** or Ctrl+C) it stops the program with Ctrl+C, as the service does.

Every unplug is a real one, by hand. The script never uses `sudo` and never touches the hardware.

**Evidence:** `docs/testing/reports/DEMO/evidence/demo-<date>-<time>/`: `summary.md` (one row per expected line:
seen and when, or missed; your words), `session.log` (every line the program printed, timestamped) and
`steps/` (each step's lines).

## The sequence (`demo_run.py --list`)

About 10 minutes with talking. The panel step is the slow one: the panel is only called lost after 30 s
without its heartbeat.

| # | Step | Tell the panel | Do | The program should, within |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Normal use, everything connected | The strip along the bottom shows every part working; the device announces what the camera sees. | Point the camera at the room; have someone step in front of it, or walk it past a chair. | an object announced (30 s); no failsafe alert (15 s) |
| 2 | Camera out and back | Pull the camera: the wearer is told at once, every 10 s, with two long pulses; it recovers by itself. | Pull the camera's USB cable. | status: camera NOT CONNECTED (10 s); "Critical Error: Camera cannot be reached" (10 s); camera pulses (two long) (10 s); the sentence repeated 10 s later (25 s) |
|  |  |  | Plug the camera back in. | status: camera connected (40 s); "Camera has been reconnected" (40 s) |
| 3 | Glasses board out and back | Unplug the vibration board: the wearer hears that vibration is not working; on return, two short pulses. | Unplug the glasses board's cable. | the Pi notices no answer (10 s); "Vibration is not working" (10 s); status: glasses NOT RESPONDING (10 s) |
|  |  |  | Plug the glasses board's cable back in. | the board answers again (20 s); reconnect pulses (two short) (20 s); "Everything is connected" (20 s) |
| 4 | Earphones out and back | Without earphones nothing can be heard, so the device tells the wearer by vibration: strong, weak, three times. | Unplug the earphones. | the Pi notices the sound card is gone (6 s); earphone pulses (strong, weak, x3) (6 s); status: earphones MISSING (6 s) |
|  |  |  | Plug the earphones back in. | the Pi finds them again (10 s); reconnect pulses (two short) (10 s); "Everything is connected" (10 s) |
| 5 | Control panel out and back | Unplug the control panel: after 30 s without its heartbeat the wearer is told, and the settings stay as they were. | Unplug the control panel's wire. The sentence comes 20 to 31 s later. | the Pi notices the heartbeat stopped (40 s); "Controller board cannot be reached. Using the current settings." (40 s); status: panel NOT RESPONDING (40 s) |
|  |  |  | Plug the control panel back in. It is noticed with its next heartbeat, up to 10 s. | the heartbeat is back (15 s); "Everything is connected" (15 s) |
| 6 | Camera covered, and facing a bright light | A covered or blinded camera sees no obstacles, so the wearer is warned; the numbers top right are what it measures. | Cover the lens with your hand, and keep it covered. | status: view BLOCKED OR TOO DARK (8 s); "Camera is blocked or it is too dark. Obstacle warnings may be missing." (8 s) |
|  |  |  | Uncover the lens. | "Camera view is back." (8 s); status: view clear (8 s) |
|  |  |  | Hold a phone's flash right at the lens. | status: view TOO BRIGHT (8 s); "Camera is facing very bright light. Obstacle warnings may be missing." (8 s) |
|  |  |  | Take the flash away. | "Camera view is back." (8 s) |
| 7 | The performance chart | 20 minutes at full load: 30 frames per second, the AI chip at 85 %, no throttling. | Open the chart on the screen: `docs/testing/reports/T-15/evidence/perf-npu-20min-with-npu.png` (and the live NPU monitor: `perf-npu-20min-screen-hailortcli-monitor.png`) | — |

**Why this order:** each part comes back before the next goes, so every return can say "Everything is connected"
(D-17: only when nothing else is out). The earphones go after the glasses because a sentence said while they are
out cannot be heard. The lens step needs the camera picture, so it comes after the camera is back.

**The windows are generous on purpose** (the program's own times: the camera alert at once and every 10 s, the
glasses after about 2.5 s, the earphones within 1 to 2 s, the panel 20 to 31 s, the camera view after 3 checks
of 1 s). A `MISS` means "not within the window", so look at `session.log` before calling it a failure.

**What the script cannot tell:** whether a sentence was *heard* (it sees the speech worker start it) and whether a
pattern was *felt* (it sees the pattern start). That is what your words after each step are for. Step 1's object
announcement depends on the scene; with nothing the model knows in view, it says `MISS`.

## Tested (`tests/test_demo_run.py`, 21 tests, no device)

- **The sentences** it listens for are pinned to the program's own constants (`core/connections.py`,
  `core/camera_view.py`, `pipeline/app.py`), and its status patterns to the status board's real lines, so a
  changed sentence fails a test instead of the demo.
- **The step engine:** a line counts with its delay; a repeat needs both lines; an absent line passes only when it
  never comes; a reaction before the Enter counts (negative time), one after the window does not, and one from
  before the prompt does not; a step that went well moves on at once; none waits past its window.
- **Whole runs** against a stand-in program that prints the real program's lines when the "presenter" acts: the
  evidence folder, the live lines, a missed line failing the run, skip and early finish (the program is still
  stopped, exit 0), a step skipped for a missing part, an unplug noticed before the Enter.
- **Before it starts:** it refuses while the boot service runs, while another copy runs, or without a camera, and
  finds missing boards.
- **Mutation check:** 12 changes, each removing one behaviour in a scratch copy. The first run left one survivor
  (a skip still passing as one); the test now checks each answer's own row. **All 12 caught**
  (`evidence/F8-mutations.txt`).
- Full suites: see DEMO-HANDOFF.md, "test baseline".

**Found on the way:** the first draft started counting at the Enter, but the camera alert is said the instant the
cable comes out, often before the presenter presses Enter, so that step would have shown a `MISS` on a device that
worked. It now counts from the moment the prompt appears and times from the Enter.
