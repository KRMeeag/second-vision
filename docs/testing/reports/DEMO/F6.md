## Behaviour to know

- **When it is noticed.** The panel sends `B:alive` every 10 s. The worker says "lost" when more than 30 s have
  passed since the last one. If you unplug it, the sentence comes **20 to 31 s later** (the last beat was up
  to 10 s before the unplug). It comes back with the **next** beat, up to 10 s after you plug it in.
- **Only `B:alive` counts as a sign of life**, as before. Settings and mode lines do not reset the clock.
- **A panel that never beats at all** is reported 31 s after the port opened, and welcomed by the first beat.
- **A port that cannot be opened is not opened again.** The alert then lasts the whole run, with no return.
  This does not matter for the panel on the GPIO UART (`/dev/ttyAMA3`, which always exists once the overlay is
  loaded, so pulling the wire shows up as silence, and that recovers). It would for a USB-serial panel.
- **No panel asked for** (no `--config-port`): nothing is said, and in `main.py` the worker is not even started.
- **Boot.** The panel and the Pi start together and the worker's clock starts when the port opens, so the first
  beat (within 10 s) comes well inside the 30 s. No false alarm at boot is expected; a reboot with the panel
  plugged in would show it.
- No vibration, in either direction (REQUIREMENTS section 8).

## Device check (batched session)

The boot service must be stopped first (the user stops it or approves it); the panel reader needs
`--config-port`, which `scripts/sv-main.sh` passes. Run the program from this worktree (`DEMO-CHECKLIST.md`,
Part B). Then:

1. Unplug the panel's wire (or its power). Wait.
   - Expect "Controller board cannot be reached. Using the current settings." **20 to 31 s later**.
2. Turn the speech switch off (if you can reach it; otherwise skip). The alert still comes (D41).
3. Plug it back in.
   - Expect "Everything is connected" within about 10 s.
   - The panel re-announces its whole state every 10 s (`config_reader.py`), so a rocker or knob moved meanwhile takes effect with its next report.
4. Unplug it again with the camera also out (`usb_sim.py detach camera --for 90`): plug the panel back first and
   expect "Controller board connected", not "Everything is connected".
5. Optional: leave it out for 5 minutes and hear the repeat.
6. Write in `F6.md` what was heard. The log lines to look for are `[CONFIG] No heartbeat from the control panel`,
   `[ALERT] raised 'panel-missing'`, `[CONFIG] Control panel is back` and `[ALERT] raised 'panel-reconnected'`.
